Note on Rendering

Open Babel's POV-Ray export generates 3D ball-and-stick models but does not visually distinguish double bonds from single bonds in the final image. The structural geometry is correct, but C=O carbonyl groups and other multiple bonds appear as standard cylinders.

Example SMILES for Testing:

  • Benzene: c1ccccc1 - Simple aromatic ring
  • Caffeine: CN1C=NC2=C1C(=O)N(C(=O)N2C)C - Complex heterocycle
  • Aspirin: CC(=O)Oc1ccccc1C(=O)O - Pharmaceutical compound
  • Methane: C - Simplest organic molecule
